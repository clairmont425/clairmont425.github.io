How we pick leaders

Traders are ranked on the overall-monthly-PnL leaderboard (/v2/leaderboard) and then gated on:

  • Win rate ≥ 55% on settled markets (not realized PnL alone)
  • Closed trades ≥ 20 so win-rate is statistically meaningful
  • ROI > 0 over the evaluated window

Those who pass become the follow-list. Their WR / ROI / T (total closed) are shown under each trader's name on the row.

The score (0–100) column

A composite I compute per position to surface the trades most worth copying:

  • Trader win rate (0–30): 60% → 10, 70% → 20, 80% → 30
  • Size vs trader's typical (0–25): bigger-than-usual = conviction
  • Price sweet spot (0–15): peaks at 0.50 where edge is clearest
  • PnL stage (0–15): sweet spot is +0 to +30% (validated, not run yet)
On combos / parlays

Every entry is an individual bet. Polymarket.com doesn't offer combo/parlay orders — that's a polymarket.us-only product. The closest signal here is BASKET markers on positions in a mutually exclusive outcome set (e.g., "who wins the nomination?"). Multiple legs of a basket from one trader is hedging within one event, not a parlay.

How the scanner works

Every run, the scanner pulls every live NBA + NFL game market on polymarket.us (~25,000 markets) and every live game's odds from DraftKings via ESPN (free, no key). For each Polymarket market, it computes a "fair" probability from DK's devigged price and compares that fair price to Polymarket's actual price. Only markets where the two disagree by more than the edge threshold are listed below.

What the numbers mean
  • Market = Polymarket's current devigged price (the probability the market is pricing in).
  • Fair = DraftKings' devigged price (the probability a sharp sportsbook is pricing in). This is the reference.
  • Edge (pp) = Fair − Market in percentage points. Higher = Polymarket is more wrong relative to DK. Sort column default.
  • EV % = Expected Value on a $100 stake, after fees. +5% means if you bet $100 and the fair probability is right, you'd make an average of $5. Positive = profitable in the long run. Negative = you'd lose money on average even though you might win single bets.
  • EV $/100 = EV % expressed in dollars on a $100 bet. Same number, different unit.
How often it refreshes

The daily Windows scheduled task Polymarket-Leaderboard-Dash-Daily runs at 07:30 AM local time, refreshes DK odds from ESPN, rescans all Polymarket markets, regenerates this page, and pushes to clairmont425.github.io. You can trigger a manual refresh any time from the terminal with python polymarket-sports/scanner/value_scanner.py. Current page timestamp: 2026-11-16 15:31Z.
